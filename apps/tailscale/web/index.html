Tailscale

Tailscale connects the NAS to your private Tailscale network (tailnet), so you can reach it securely from anywhere. It is set up over SSH:

tailscale up              # log in - open the link it shows to add the NAS to your tailnet
tailscale status          # show the NAS's Tailscale address and connected devices
tailscale ip -4           # show the NAS's Tailscale IPv4 address

The NAS stays logged in and reconnects by itself after a restart. Options such as sharing your home network (--advertise-routes) or acting as an exit node (--advertise-exit-node) are added to tailscale up, or changed later with tailscale set.

See the Tailscale documentation for more.
