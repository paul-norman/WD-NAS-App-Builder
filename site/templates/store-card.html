The Store $version

Install the Store first: it replaces the NAS's Apps page and installs, updates and removes everything else - apps and Docker recipes - from the NAS's own dashboard. It checks every download, and can keep the apps you choose up to date by itself, overnight.

Download the Store About the Store

Pick your NAS above for its download.

  1. Download the Store's package for your NAS (a .bin file).
  2. In the NAS's dashboard, open Apps, then Install an app manually, and choose that file.
  3. Open the Store (it's the Apps page now): every app and recipe here is a click away, with its updates.
